gymcatch · Brick-and-mortar · https://gymcatch.com/

Gymcatch is an affordable, UK-origin booking and class-management system for fitness and wellness businesses — fitness/dance/Pilates/yoga instructors, PTs, gyms, studios, martial arts, and sports clubs. Its core pitch is reducing admin and increasing revenue through in-person, live-virtual, and on-demand class scheduling, bookings, passes/memberships, and payments, with client-facing web/iOS/Android apps. Pricing is a low flat monthly base plus à-la-carte "bolt-ons," so businesses only pay for the features they use.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is its modular bolt-on pricing: a very low £12.75/mo base with individual £3/mo add-ons (memberships, courses, discount codes, waivers, website embed, etc.) so solo operators pay only for what they use — anti-incumbent pricing aimed at the budget-conscious single-instructor segment.

Segment: Small independent fitness & wellness operators — instructors, PTs, studios, dance/Pilates/yoga/martial-arts businesses, and small community sports centers (mostly UK + international).

Pricing: From £12.75/mo +VAT base with 1 month free (no card required, cancel anytime). Core includes unlimited bookings/customers/team, scheduling, virtual, passes, notifications, community, referrals, and 0%-cut payment processing (fees excluded). Bolt-ons are £3/mo +VAT each (memberships, courses, discount codes, waivers, website embed, auto credit-backs, booking reminders). Supports GymcatchPay, Stripe, GoCardless.

Traction: "Excellent" rating based on 210 Capterra reviews; repeatedly praised for customer service, ease of use, and value. Testimonials cite multi-year use (4.5+ years), £900/yr savings vs. prior systems, and 90% paperwork reduction. Partnered with CIMSPA, FitPro, and ASFA. ©2026 footer; actively shipping (bolt-ons, PAR-Q roadmap).

Maturity: Established, mature niche product — several years in market, broad review base, international pricing in 30+ countries, and ongoing feature development. Clearly a small/mid SMB-focused tool rather than an enterprise platform.

recess · Brick-and-mortar · https://recess.tv/

Recess is a free-to-use studio and gym management platform funded by a ~5% member-paid service fee at checkout (which also covers card/bank processing). Its pitch is "your software should be doing more of the work" — an operations platform that doesn't just report problems but acts on them: an AI agent answers leads and books trials, failed payments self-recover, cancelled classes auto-source cover, and quiet members get auto-chased into win-back sequences. It bundles two-way texting/email, a branded member app, VOD, challenges/streaks, and a Business Health financial dashboard, and does full done-for-you migration off ~20 named incumbents.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is action-not-reporting automation: the software actively does the operational work (AI agent answers after-hours leads, auto-recovers failed payments, auto-finds coach cover via text broadcast, auto-runs win-back sequences). Paired with inverted economics ($0/mo, funded by ~5% member checkout fee) and a done-for-you migration off 20 named incumbents.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar multi-discipline studios and gyms — martial arts/dojos, dance, gymnastics, pilates, barre, yoga, CrossFit, HIIT, boxing/MMA, personal training — including multi-location and franchise groups up to ~100 sites. Heavy emphasis on family/youth-program billing and seasons/camps.

Pricing: $0/month, no platform fee, no per-feature or per-location charge, no setup fee, no contract. Every feature included from day one, unlimited staff logins, unlimited text/email, unlimited video storage, branded app, and team-run migration. Monetized by a ~5% service fee paid by the member at checkout, out of which Recess absorbs card/bank processing. Explicitly flags cases where a flat-fee competitor would be cheaper (high-volume/low-transaction studios).

Traction: Actively hiring part-time field sales reps across 11 cities (field-sales GTM motion). Claims to migrate studios off ~20 named incumbents "every week." Business Health dashboard noted as "rolling out to studios now" (feature mid-rollout). Multi-location/multi-state operators and franchise groups cited as customers. No hard customer counts or revenue figures published.

Maturity: Growth-stage and feature-broad: full ops suite plus automation and a branded app are live, with the Business Health financials feature explicitly still rolling out. The field-sales hiring across 11 cities and weekly-migration claims suggest an active scaling push rather than early seed stage.

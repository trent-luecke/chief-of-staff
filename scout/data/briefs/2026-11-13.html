AI Coaching Platform Tailored to Your Company · Brick-and-mortar · https://rocky.ai/

Rocky.ai is a no-code "Coaching Operating System" that lets coaches, HR/L&D teams, and organisations build and deploy white-label AI coaching, mentoring, training, and roleplay agents grounded in their own methodology and content. It bundles multi-agent AI coaches, conversation roleplays with rubric scoring, self-assessments (e.g. DISC), interactive courses (SCORM in/out), cross-session memory/follow-through, voice and talking avatars, a coach panel with skill analytics, and enterprise controls (GDPR, SSO, PII filtering) — all configurable from one admin studio with per-client sub-brands.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge": weighted retrieval that forces the AI to coach strictly inside your uploaded methodology/frameworks (an "AI that coaches your method" vs. one that merely "read your book"), delivered as a no-code white-label app live in hours with per-client sub-brands — positioning methodology as the one thing AI can't commoditise.

Segment: Online coaching / learning & development — AI-powered white-label coaching platform for coaches, mentors, and HR/L&D teams (category bucket B: online coaching). Not a brick-and-mortar gym-ops tool.

Pricing: Business tiers: Small Teams $99/mo (5 users), Medium Teams $199/mo (20 users, add own store + Stripe), Large Teams $1–10/user/mo (volume/annual). A $29/mo build tier to construct the white-label before onboarding clients; 14-day free trial. Separate consumer app: Free, $9.99/mo individual, $19.90/seat professional. Tiered + per-seat scaling (not OS's flat single-price model).

Traction: Techstars-backed; recognized by TechCrunch Disrupt (Top Pick), BSH Future Home, Digital Magics, Lazio Innova; AppSumo Select 4.8/5. Live iOS/Android/web consumer app with App Store/Google Play reviews. Founder sits on the ICF working group defining AI coaching standards. Appears to be an established, funded early-growth company.

Maturity: Growth-stage / established — funded accelerator pedigree, multi-platform shipping product (iOS, Android, web), mature enterprise posture (GDPR, SSO, SCORM, 29 languages), and a full business + consumer product line. Leads with "NEW" Coaching OS repositioning, so actively evolving.

Feature set:

  • No-code white-label AI coaching app builder (custom domain, logo, sub-brands per client)
  • Multi-agent AI coaches that switch method/style/tone, grounded in your frameworks (weighted retrieval)
  • AI roleplays with 40+ templates + custom scenarios, scored against your rubric
  • Conversation assessments — every chat/roleplay scored against your criteria with instant feedback
  • Self-assessments (e.g. DISC) that feed the next coaching conversation
  • Interactive AI courses with coaching chats, roleplays, quizzes; SCORM in & out
  • Cross-session memory, goals, nudges, check-ins and follow-through
  • Voice & talking avatars (speak out loud, interrupt naturally, localized)
  • Coach panel with skill-mastery, practice-frequency, scores and sentiment analytics per team
  • Creator Studio: import YouTube/PDFs/articles, build phased programs, tune coach personality
  • Enterprise controls: GDPR-safe EU hosting, PII filtering, SSO, HR-system integration, 29 languages incl. RTL
  • Built-in store with Stripe subscriptions, trials and vouchers (Medium tier)
  • ICF-aligned coaching guardrails, human-supervised AI coaching

clubautomation · Brick-and-mortar · https://clubautomation.com/

Daxko Club Automation is an enterprise/mid-market gym and club management platform that unifies billing, scheduling, member management, CRM, payments, marketing, and reporting into one data platform built to run an entire multi-service club. Part of the Daxko family, it bundles core ops (Club Automation), a sales CRM (Engage Pro), payments/billing (Daxko Payments), and done-for-you digital marketing/website services. Positioning is heavily "all-in-one consolidation" for larger multi-location clubs and fitness centers.

⚡ Standout wedge: Deep, enterprise-grade consolidation under one data platform — core ops + a full sales CRM (Engage Pro) with AI-powered lead nurture + integrated payments with revenue recovery + done-for-you digital marketing/website/SEO/ad services, all backed by an open partner integration ecosystem. It's a true multi-location "command center" aimed at larger clubs.

Segment: Mid-market to enterprise multi-service clubs and fitness centers — health clubs, athletic clubs, golf/fitness clubs, and large franchise gyms (Crunch, Gold's Gym, Midtown Athletic Club). Explicitly "clubs & fitness centers of all sizes & services," but the big-logo roster and CRM/marketing depth skew toward sophisticated, multi-location operators.

Pricing: No pricing published on the scraped pages — demo-request / sales-led motion ("Try a demo"). Modular product bundling (Club Automation, Engage Pro, Payments, Marketing Services), consistent with enterprise/mid-market custom quoting rather than a transparent flat price.

Multiple G2 Leader / Mid-Market Leader / Momentum Leader badges (Winter–Summer 2026) and Capterra 2025 Shortlist. Customer-story testimonials. Backed by Daxko, an established fitness-software company.

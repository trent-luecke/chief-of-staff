Gym Management Software · Brick-and-mortar · https://gymdesk.com/

Gymdesk (formerly Martial Arts on Rails) is an all-in-one gym management platform for brick-and-mortar studios — martial arts / BJJ first, but also fitness gyms, yoga, Pilates, CrossFit, dance, and membership clubs. Covers memberships, automated billing, attendance/check-in, class booking, a free gym website, POS, marketing, reporting, member mobile app, and smart-lock facility access. Positioning leans hard on honesty (no hidden fees), painless migration, same-day human support, and giving owner-operators their evenings back from admin.

⚡ Standout wedge: Weird wedge = martial-arts-native vertical depth: built-in belt/rank/promotion tracking with per-program rank progression and configurable qualification rules (classes, hours, attendance, skills, age) that auto-flags who's ready to promote. Combined with strong migration handholding (saved-card transfer) and same-day human support as the trust story.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gym/studio ops (bucket A). Owner-operator small-to-mid studios, martial-arts-native (BJJ/MMA/karate/taekwondo/Muay Thai/judo) expanding to general fitness, yoga/Pilates, CrossFit, dance, gymnastics, membership clubs. 4,500+ gyms across 34 countries.

Pricing: Flat, all-features-included, scale-by-active-member-count (not by feature). $75/mo (≤50 members), $100/mo (51–100), $150/mo (101–200), $200/mo (201–400), custom Enterprise above. Unlimited staff accounts on every tier; no per-staff fees, no feature gating, no marketing/website/app add-ons. Month-to-month, no contract, 30-day free trial no credit card. Bring your own processor at your own negotiated rates with no Gymdesk markup, or use Gymdesk Payments; text/processing billed at cost.

Traction: 4,500+ gym owners across 34 countries. Hundreds of 5-star reviews on Capterra, GetApp, and Software Advice (legacy "Martial Arts onRails" listings). Multiple named martial-arts testimonials (Alliance JJ, King Tiger, Sasaki Judo, OCTA BJJ, etc.).

Maturity: Established / mature. Real logo wall, sizable customer base across 34 countries, mature review-site presence, multiple payment-processor integrations, and a rebrand from "Martial Arts on Rails" — a product several years into market, not a launch.

Feature set:

  • Memberships — unlimited plans, ranks, programs, family accounts
  • Billing — automatic collection, failed-payment recovery, overdue alerts
  • Attendance — check-in via kiosk, name search, barcode, or phone
  • Booking — member-managed class schedule from phone
  • Free gym website (or embed schedule/forms/login into existing site)
  • Point-of-sale — retail sales + inventory tracking
  • Marketing — email, SMS, referral programs, automated lead follow-ups
  • Reporting — revenue, per-membership revenue, retention, attendance, member growth trends
  • Member mobile app — check in, book, track progress
  • Facility Access — app + smart-lock 24/7 door access
  • Integrations with existing tools
  • Payment processors — Gymdesk Payments, Stripe, Square, Authorize.net, GoCardless, Ezypay (bring your own, no markup)
  • Belt/rank/promotion tracking with configurable qualification rules and promotion-ready flags
  • Automation flows / lead nurture (email + SMS + in-app) with contact tagging
  • Guided migration with a specialist incl. saved-card transfer

AI Boutique Fitness Software for Studios · Brick-and-mortar · https://joinzipper.com/

Zipper is an "operating system" for boutique fitness studios — an all-in-one platform that replaces the scheduler, email tool, website, CRM, and lead management with a single system. It gives equal weight to group classes AND appointments (privates, duets, semi-privates, events), and ships with a branded client app, website builder, memberships/billing, 2-way SMS + email marketing automation, a unified omni-channel inbox, and an AI "chat with your data" layer backed by a per-studio data warehouse. Positioned explicitly against Mindbody, Momence, WellnessLiving, Glofox, Marianatek, Pike13, and Vagaro, with anti-incumbent month-to-month pricing and no consumer marketplace that poaches clients.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is the AI "Chat With Your Data" layer: every studio gets its own secure data warehouse and an AI that answers plain-English questions ("Which clients are at risk of churning?", "What was my busiest class last month?") pulled straight from its own numbers — and can draft win-back messages off the same signal. Packaged as a headline pitch, not a checkbox.

Segment: Boutique fitness & wellness studios — Pilates, yoga, boutique fitness, personal training, sauna/cold plunge, martial arts/BJJ, and dance. Explicitly targets studios that mix group classes with private/semi-private appointments. Sizes from solo instructor (up to 100 members) to multi-location.

Pricing: Month-to-month, no annual contracts / setup fees. Starter $139/mo (solo, up to 100 members, 1% payment fee, 1 staff, basic automation). Studio $299/mo (up to 300 members, 0% payment fee, 10 staff, advanced automation/1,000 SMS, substitution mgmt). Growth $499/mo (unlimited staff, 10,000 SMS, premium onboarding, API access, +$129/mo per additional location). Scale-by-size-not-feature; anti-incumbent positioning.

Traction: Named studio logos across Pilates, yoga, boutique fitness, martial arts, CrossFit, and dance (CS Pilates, RockFit, Corner Boxing Club, Kasa Yoga, CrossFit 557, Stella Pilates, etc.). Multiple five-star testimonials explicitly citing switches from Mindbody, Glofox, MyBestStudio, Marianatek, Arketa, Momence, WellnessLiving. Emphasis on responsive support that turns customer suggestions into shipped features — reads like a growing, early-stage but real product with real customers.

Maturity: Emerging / early-growth. Modern feature-complete "OS" positioning with real customer proof and active feature shipping, but small studio-scale customer base and a young brand still defining itself primarily against incumbents.

Feature set:

  • Unified calendar for group classes, privates, duets, semi-privates, and events with customizable rules and shared client profiles
  • Memberships & billing — hybrid/combo plans, auto-renewing (bottomless) packs, class packs, unlimited, guest passes, family/household sharing of credits
  • True white-label branded client booking & check-in app
  • 2-way SMS texting plus multi-step automated marketing journeys and email campaigns
  • Unified inbox aggregating email, SMS, Instagram DMs, and Facebook Messenger with every conversation logged to the client profile
  • Website builder — fully branded, conversion-oriented, included (or embed booking widgets into an existing site)
  • Per-studio data warehouse with AI 'chat with your data' (churn-risk surfacing, revenue trend & forecast, top clients, busiest class)
  • Financial + customer + registration analytics dashboard (revenue, refunds, new customers/leads, booked/active/canceled/waitlisted/no-show)
  • CRM, forms & waivers, lead management
  • Substitution management for instructors
  • Guided migration from Mindbody/Momence/WellnessLiving/Glofox/Pike13/Vagaro incl. Stripe card-data transfer
  • API access (OpenAPI spec) on top tier
  • Multi-location support

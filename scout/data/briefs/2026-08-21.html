managememberships · Brick-and-mortar · https://managememberships.com/

ManageMemberships is a cloud-based, all-in-one membership management platform for independent gyms, fitness studios, MMA/dojos, yoga/dance studios, and even gun ranges. Its core pitch is revenue recovery: it consolidates automated billing (Stripe Connect), digital ID cards, physical door access control (custom Raspberry Pi/ESP32 hardware), class/event scheduling, waivers, CRM, and email/SMS campaigns into one system so gyms stop leaking money to failed payments, propped doors, and expired-but-still-active members.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is DIY physical door access control built on custom hardware (Raspberry Pi controller + ESP32 relay modules) enabling phone/QR 24/7 door unlock with camera-sync tailgating detection and offline cached credentials — access control sold as an $89/mo add-on rather than a third-party integration.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gym/studio ops (Bucket A) — independent, small-to-growing membership-based facilities (gyms, MMA/dojo, yoga, dance, gun ranges), with a multi-location Enterprise tier.

Pricing: Tiered, scale-by-size with some feature-gating: Free ($0/mo, up to 50 members, 6% processing fee), Starter ($29/mo, 150 members, 5%), Core ($99/mo, unlimited members, 3.5%, most popular), Enterprise (custom). Add-ons: Door Access $89/mo, POS $59/mo. Free migration included. Note: charges a processing fee on top of Stripe — contrast with OS's 0% cut.

Traction: Self-reported: 20,000+ members managed, 12k+ bookings, 28k+ secure entries, $1.5M+ processed, 99.99% uptime, 5.0 rating on Google and G2. Named clients/testimonials: Raise the Bar Performance, Mayhem MMA, The Pit Barbell Club, Piasa Armory, Lawman Tactical. Founder-operator/hands-on sales feel (direct email jon@ and personal meeting links).

Maturity: Early/growth-stage but functionally broad — a real, shipping product with a live demo, multiple named clients, and a full pricing/feature matrix. Small scale (tens of thousands of members, ~$1.5M processed) but covers ops end-to-end.

connected-performance · Brick-and-mortar · https://connected-performance.com/

Connected Performance is a baseball-specific remote training service plus an accompanying coaching software product ("Connect Platform"). The company runs its own remote pitching/performance coaching operation and sells the Connect Platform to other coaches and sport-performance organizations as an all-in-one training app for building programs, tracking athlete development, and running team operations. The software is deeply verticalized around baseball/pitching development (arm health, pitch command, biomechanics).

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is deep baseball verticalization: a baseball-specific exercise library, a Command Tracker, and native Nextiles smart-fabric sleeve integration that auto-uploads arm-health data into the app. This vertical hardware/data depth is unusual and genuinely inspiring.

Segment: Sport-performance / S&C coaching software, hyper-verticalized on baseball & pitching development — sold to pitching coaches, college programs, and remote/in-person training facilities. Overlaps directly with TeamBuildr OS's core ICP (strength & conditioning / sport-performance).

Pricing: Tiered by athlete count, published on a comparison table: ~$200/20 athletes (base) up to $400/20 athletes; unlimited plan "roughly 20k"; competitor column references show $250/month and $400/month for 500 athletes, and "upwards of $60k a year" for enterprise alternatives. Pricing is size-scaled, quote-driven via a form/demo. More opaque and higher-touch than OS's flat published $200/mo.

Traction: Strong vertical proof: 39 athletes drafted/signed since 2021, 6 top-10-round draft picks, 83+ college commits, 996+ athletes on the Connect Platform. Named testimonials from MLB-org pitchers (Rays, Braves, White Sox) and college/facility coaches (Lander University, Revolution Sports Performance, Martin Pitching Performance). Active blog with thought-leadership content.

Maturity: Growing, niche/early-to-mid stage. Real customer logos and athlete counts, but small and tightly focused on baseball; some pages 404 and pricing table copy is rough/duplicated, suggesting a lean team still polishing the product-marketing.

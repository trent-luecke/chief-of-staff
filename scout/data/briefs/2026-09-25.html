rhinofit · Brick-and-mortar · https://rhinofit.ca/

RhinoFit is a flat-rate gym management software from a small (fourteen-year-old) team in Clearwater, Florida. It handles billing, class scheduling, waivers, POS, member management, and 24/7 door access for independent gyms, CrossFit boxes, dojos, and sports clubs. Its core pitch is anti-incumbent simplicity: one flat $57/month price with unlimited members, a $0 tier for true start-ups, and human phone support that anchors the whole brand ("a group of rhinos is called a crash").

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is the radical human-support + flat-rate model: real people (including a code-writing CEO) answer an 800 number in one building, paired with a genuinely flat $57 unlimited-members price and a $0 start-up tier. Support-as-differentiator is the whole positioning, not a feature footnote.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar independent gym/studio ops (Bucket A). Serves small independent gyms, CrossFit boxes, dojos, martial arts, sports clubs, and fitness studios — especially budget-conscious start-ups and 24-hour access facilities.

Pricing: One flat $57/month with unlimited members — no tiers, no per-member fees, email & SMS included with no usage fee. Free 30-day trial, no credit card. $0/month start-up plan for 20 or fewer active users. 24/7 door access & branded app are paid add-ons. No contract; cancel by phone.

Traction: Operating since 2012 (14 years), 4.8★ across 201 Google reviews. Multiple Capterra awards (Top 10 Service, Fast Implementation, User Experience, 2025 Crystal Award). Small single-building team in Clearwater, FL. Serves gyms across the U.S. and Canada; explicitly does not support clients outside those two countries.

Maturity: Mature, stable small-business incumbent — 14 years in market, steady growth, established review base and award history. Not a venture-scale or fast-moving player; deliberately small and support-focused.

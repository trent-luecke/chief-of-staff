goteamup · Brick-and-mortar · https://goteamup.com/

TeamUp (goteamup.com) is an all-in-one gym and studio management platform for brick-and-mortar fitness businesses — yoga studios, CrossFit gyms, Pilates studios, personal training, martial arts, and general fitness centers. It centers on scheduling, membership/billing, member experience/communication, and "built for and by fitness professionals" positioning, with strong UK/global traction and a free-data-migration + 1-on-1 onboarding switching pitch.

⚡ Standout wedge: Frictionless switching as a wedge: free data migration from other platforms + 1-on-1 onboarding + responsive multi-channel support (live chat, Help Center, AI assistant) — removing the pain of leaving an incumbent.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar group-fitness studios and gyms (yoga, Pilates, CrossFit, martial arts, PT, general fitness centers) — "Bucket A" facility ops, heavily UK/global.

Pricing: Not published on the scraped pages — demo-request / "Try it Free" signup gated; no transparent price shown (contrast with OS's published single $200/mo).

Traction: Strong, mature traction: trusted by named studios/gyms globally (GymBox, Hotpod Yoga, Hyrox, Basi, Graft), 4.8 on Capterra, 4.6 on G2, multiple testimonials, "thousands of studios and gyms." Heavy UK/worldwide footprint.

Maturity: Mature, established player — broad vertical coverage, named enterprise/brand logos, strong review-site presence, and polished switching/onboarding machinery.

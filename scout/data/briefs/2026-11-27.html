bridgeathletic · Brick-and-mortar · https://bridgeathletic.com/

BridgeAthletic is a strength & conditioning software platform for building, delivering, and tracking periodized, %-based training programs across pro sports, collegiate, tactical, youth, independent trainers, and gyms. It pairs deep programming (exercise library, templates, prescribed-vs-actual load monitoring) with remote/in-weight-room delivery to athlete phones and tablets. Recently acquired by/joined VALD, it now connects objective performance measurement to individualized programming, and has expanded into Learning (100+ courses, custom content, compliance/onboarding) and Insights (surveys, wellness check-ins).

⚡ Standout wedge: Deep, professional periodized programming built for serious athletes (prescribed-vs-actual load monitoring), now embedded in the VALD measurement ecosystem connecting objective testing to individualized programming.

Segment: Strength & conditioning for athletic organizations (pro/college/national teams), tactical units, youth orgs, independent trainers, and gyms/clubs — performance-training-first, serving OS's core S&C ICP.

Pricing: No pricing published on scraped pages; demo-request / sales-led model. Segmented by organization type (enterprise/team-oriented), consistent with scale-by-size positioning.

Traction: Strong, mature traction: 5M+ workouts, 6,000+ sports teams, 200,000+ athletes, 5,000+ personal trainers, 50+ tactical orgs, 100+ national teams. Logos include NFL, NHL, NBA, MLS Players Association, LA Rams, USC, and multiple universities. Joined the VALD ecosystem.

Maturity: Mature, enterprise-grade incumbent; long-established with elite pro/college clientele and a recent VALD acquisition expanding the ecosystem.

kahunas · Brick-and-mortar · https://kahunas.io/

Kahunas is an all-in-one online coaching platform for personal trainers and fitness coaches, consolidating programs, nutrition, check-ins, habits, payments, communities, courses, and a branded client app into one place. It leans hard on AI (Whim assistant + intelligent insights), an instant installable web app (no app store), and per-seat pricing that drops as you scale.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "instant app" wedge: an installable web app clients add in 3 taps, explicitly positioned against app-store removal risk and 30% store fees — paired with a deeply integrated, client-aware AI assistant (Whim) that reads real check-in/workout/nutrition data and drafts plans for coach review.

Segment: Online coaching (bucket B) — solo-to-scaling fitness/PT coaches and coaching businesses selling 1:1 and group coaching, nutrition, challenges, and courses to clients via a branded app.

Pricing: Per-seat, scale-by-size pricing: $5.00/seat down to $0.50/seat at 1,000+ seats (seats = active + paused clients + non-archived team; archived clients free). 0% sales commission; coaches keep payment revenue via Stripe/PayPal. 14-day free trial plus a 3-month money-back guarantee. Black Friday: up to 50% off with 4- or 12-month upfront. AI assistant included from 50 seats.

Traction: Claims 15,000+ coaches and 1,000,000+ users, "Recommended by PureGym." Named coach testimonials with social handles. Founder ("Mark") offers a personal same-day refund guarantee — a founder-operator trust signal.

Maturity: Mature and actively shipping — broad feature surface, newly launched AI assistant (Whim) and instant web app, Black Friday pricing motion, and a large claimed user base indicate a well-established product past MVP.

Feature set:

  • Workout program builder with 1,700+ exercise library, volume-by-muscle-group, set-by-set review
  • Metrics charting (check-ins and habits over 7/30/90 days)
  • Habit tracking with adherence and streaks
  • Nutrition meal plans with per-meal macros from 1.5M+ foods
  • Roadmaps — seasonal periodization in phases on one timeline
  • Communities (group rooms with moderation/review queue)
  • Education — courses with modules/lessons as a revenue line
  • Challenges — free/paid group programs with weigh-ins and voting
  • Content library (reusable videos/articles/guides by package)
  • Intelligent insights — AI triage of who needs attention and why
  • Whim AI assistant — client-aware Q&A + drafts programs/nutrition/forms for review
  • Automations — trigger-based personalized emails and push notifications
  • Labs — client lab report upload parsed by marker
  • Branded client app (logo, color, menu)
  • Payments via Stripe/PayPal with 0% commission
  • Instant installable web app (no app store, instant updates)
  • Per-seat pricing ($5 down to $0.50/seat)

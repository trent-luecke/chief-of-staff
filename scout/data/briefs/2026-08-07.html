Strakly is a free, AI-powered gym management platform built by an indie developer (Shreyesh Kolhe) targeting small and mid-size gyms in emerging markets (India, Nigeria, Indonesia) that are priced out of $50–200/mo incumbents. Core is brick-and-mortar gym ops: client profiles, QR-code attendance, payment/subscription tracking, trainer management, multi-branch, and analytics — with a built-in AI voice assistant as the headline differentiator. Founder-operator origin story (a broken Excel sheet), completely free forever, monetization deferred to future premium features/analytics/integrations.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is the inverted economics + geography play: completely free forever, aimed at gym owners in emerging markets who are choosing between Strakly and a notebook — not between Strakly and a competitor. Plus a built-in AI voice assistant you can literally talk to about your gym data.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gym/studio ops (Bucket A) — small/mid-size general fitness gyms in underserved/emerging markets, not S&C or sport-performance.

Pricing: Completely free, forever — no trial, no freemium trap, no credit card, no setup fee. Anti-incumbent pricing built around the fact that incumbents cost $50–200/mo, impossible for an owner charging members $10–15/mo. Monetization deferred to future premium features, advanced analytics, and integrations.

Traction: Very early / indie stage. Single full-stack developer founder, Medium post (Feb 2026) with ~1 clap and 8 followers on the author account. Product is "live and gyms are using it" per copy but no named logos, counts, or metrics. Explicitly soliciting "tear the product apart" feedback from developers/founders — signals pre-scale.

Maturity: Early-stage MVP by a solo indie developer; core CRM/attendance/payments shipped, most differentiating roadmap items (Stripe, wearables, CRM, campaigns, gamification, mobile app) still "coming soon."

Feature set:

  • Client management — member profiles with personal details, plan, attendance history, payments, body measurements, progress photos
  • QR code attendance with real-time tracking
  • Subscription & payment tracking with digital records, automatic invoice generation, renewal reminders
  • Trainer management — assign trainers to clients, manage schedules, track performance, trainer-facing dashboard
  • Multi-branch support — one dashboard to switch between branches and compare performance
  • Analytics & reports — revenue trends, attendance patterns, membership growth, trainer utilization (visual)
  • AI voice assistant — ask the dashboard questions by voice (e.g. new members this month, who hasn't attended in 7 days)
  • At-risk/absent member surfacing (who's been absent for a week / at risk of dropping out)
  • Coming soon: Stripe payment integration for online billing
  • Coming soon: wearable device integration
  • Coming soon: Lead CRM and sales pipeline
  • Coming soon: automated email/SMS campaigns
  • Coming soon: gamification (challenges, leaderboards, rewards)
  • Coming soon: mobile app for trainers and members

Verdict: ➖ Different job — Strakly serves a different ICP and job than OS: general small gyms in emerging markets choosing between software and a notebook, competing on free/affordability. OS's job is consolidation + real periodized S&C programming for sport-performance facilities at a premium price — Strakly has zero programming depth and doesn't target S&C. Overlap exists in the reporting/at-risk-member and AI-assistant patterns (mined above as feature gaps), but the core hired-for job is different. Not a positioning gap OS should react to messaging-wise.

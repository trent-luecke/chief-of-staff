athletadesk · Brick-and-mortar · https://athletadesk.com/

AthletaDesk is business-management software aimed at solo personal trainers, fitness instructors, and sports coaches — the "am I a trainer or a business owner?" persona. It bundles client management, scheduling/booking, online payments, a drag-and-drop website builder, and back-office business tools (expenses, mileage, reports, payroll) into one app whose whole promise is to get an admin-averse coach "out of the office and back in the gym."

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is back-office bookkeeping built for the individual trainer: expense tracking for tax time, per-client mileage tracking, and schedule-based staff payroll — money-admin depth aimed squarely at the sole-proprietor coach, plus aggressively low flat pricing (~$16.95/mo + a few dollars per extra trainer).

Segment: Solo & small-team personal trainers, fitness instructors, and independent sport coaches — the individual/side-hustle-to-small-business end, not multi-location facilities.

Pricing: Flat, unlimited-clients, no-tier pricing: ~$16.95/month base plus ~$4.95 per each additional trainer/staff (localized to CAD/AUD/NZD/EUR/GBP/USD). Free trial. All features included; you scale by number of trainers, not by feature gate.

Traction: No customer counts, logos, testimonials, funding, or founder story on the scraped pages. Copyright "Port 443 Inc." Site content dated 2023; product appears established but low-visibility. "Packages" still marked coming soon, suggesting steady but modest development pace.

Maturity: Established, shipping product with a full feature set and multi-currency billing, but positioned at the low end (solo trainers) and small in market visibility. Mature core (clients/schedule/payments/website) with a few features still "coming soon."

Feature set:

  • Client management — all client info in one place, bulk import from a list
  • Bulk text & email to entire client base
  • Client Portal — clients self-book, cancel, pay
  • Booking & scheduling of private sessions and group classes with custom categories & locations
  • Automated text/email session reminders (no-show reduction)
  • Calendar sync to phone calendar
  • Online training via Zoom, Skype, FaceTime
  • Custom billing (session rate or monthly fee), online payments, auto-pay, late fees, discounts
  • Session packages (coming soon)
  • Website builder (drag & drop), custom sign-up forms, custom domain, embeddable widgets
  • Expense tracking for taxes
  • Mileage tracking between clients
  • Schedule-based staff payroll calculation
  • Business reports (finances, clients, and more)
  • Multi-trainer access controls
  • Integrations via Zapier, Stripe & PayPal billing

gymmaster · Brick-and-mortar · https://gymmaster.com/

GymMaster is an all-in-one gym and health club membership management platform by Treshna Enterprises (NZ/Australia support base). Its core pitch is being the only all-in-one gym management software with built-in 24/7 Bluetooth door access with no third party. Covers membership management, integrated billing, POS, online booking/scheduling, member app, online signups, lead management, and automated/AI-assisted communications. Aimed at brick-and-mortar clubs of all sizes.

⚡ Standout wedge: Built-in, first-party 24/7 Bluetooth door access control (no third-party hardware/integration) bundled into the same platform as management software — the "weird wedge." OS has NO access control, so this is a clean differentiator for GymMaster.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gym / health club facility operations (Bucket A). ICP is 24/7-access clubs and general fitness facilities of all sizes, globally distributed.

Pricing: Tiered by member count, per site, per month (ex. GST). Foundation ~USD $89/mo (100 members), Advanced ~$129/mo (400), Professional ~$209/mo (1300), Enterprise = ASK (unlimited). 10% discount for annual; door access is a paid add-on ("Software + Door Access"). No lock-in contracts, 60-day money-back guarantee. Note: OS is flat single $200/mo with no tiers — GymMaster scales price by size and gates door access.

Traction: Strong maturity signals: used in 110+ countries, 180k+ weekly active users, 3M+ memberships managed, 4.7 Capterra rating, Capterra "Best Ease of Use" and "Best Support" awards. Powers UBX and other franchise/club logos.

Maturity: Mature, established incumbent — multi-year operation (Treshna Enterprises), broad global footprint, awards, and a deep feature set. Not an emerging startup.

Feature set:

  • Built-in 24/7 Bluetooth door access control (first-party, unlimited doors)
  • Integrated billing & automated payment processing (ACH, credit card, invoicing)
  • Touch-screen Point of Sale with stock control
  • Membership management with member measurements & paperless online signups
  • Online booking & scheduling for classes, PT, pools, saunas, resources
  • Website integration with live-updating timetable for online signups
  • Member mobile app (iOS & Android) — booking, check-in, workouts, visitation
  • Staff mobile app / work-from-anywhere management

Effortless Gym & Studio Management Software · Brick-and-mortar · https://gymflow.io/

Gymflow is a UK-origin, "effortless" all-in-one gym & studio management platform pitched on simplicity, speed of setup, and support. It bundles class/appointment scheduling, membership & recurring billing, door access control, branded member/staff mobile apps, website embeds, a sales/lead pipeline, reporting, gift vouchers, and bulk email/SMS. Positioned for brick-and-mortar gyms and studios (24hr gyms, boutique studios, multi-club operators) — bucket (A) facility-ops, with no online-programming/coaching layer.

⚡ Standout wedge: Weird wedge = native door access control (auto grant/revoke + auto check-in, Kisi integration) tightly fused with memberships/bookings — the ops backbone for unstaffed/24hr gyms. Paired with a relentless "easiest-to-use / up in minutes" simplicity + support positioning.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gyms & boutique studios — 24hr access gyms, multi-club operators, general fitness/wellness studios. Facility-ops focused, not S&C programming.

Pricing: One flat plan: 30 days free then $159/mo (USD). No contracts (30-day cancel notice), no user/staff limits, all features included, unlimited support & bulk email, 100 SMS/mo. Fits category fingerprints: anti-incumbent flat-unlimited pricing + all-features-included scale-by-nothing model. Undercuts OS's published $200/mo.

Traction: 4.8/5 on G2, 4.9/5 on Capterra; "Trusted by 1,000+ Businesses Worldwide"; 99% CSAT from 1,565 interactions. Named-logo customers (24fit, REGEN London, Hybrid, Bare, Azure, Laramie, 218). Emphasis on seamless migration of billing/access and rapid onboarding. Founder/industry-operator trust signal ("built by industry experts with 10+ years experience").

Maturity: Mature, stable core ops product with weekly shipping cadence. Deep on facility ops and access control; no online programming/coaching layer at all.

Feature set:

  • Class & appointment scheduling with sellable packages, room/team availability, embeddable live schedules & booking links
  • Membership management & recurring billing with auto-retry on failed payments and member self-service (upgrade/pause/manage)
  • Door access control — auto grant/revoke based on memberships & bookings, auto check-in on arrival
  • Branded website integration — embeddable sign-up forms, live schedules, customer portal
  • Fully customisable branded members mobile app (register, purchase, book, manage, access)
  • Sales & marketing tools — built-in lead pipelines, promo codes, trials, follow-up tasks
  • Reporting & analytics — revenue, bookings, memberships, real-time trends
  • Gift vouchers — buy online, redeem for classes/PT/memberships, track sales/redemptions/balances
  • Bulk email & SMS (100 SMS/mo included) for promotions and member comms
  • Staff mobile app — business alerts, contactless payments, membership/schedule handling, key figures
  • Zapier integration and access-control integrations (e.g. Kisi)

Walla: Fitness Studio Software · Brick-and-mortar · https://www.hellowalla.com/

Walla is boutique fitness studio management software targeting brick-and-mortar boutique studios (yoga, Pilates, barre, Lagree, indoor cycling, HIIT/circuit). It pitches an AI-powered all-in-one platform combining studio management, client experience, reporting, sales & marketing, branded apps, custom websites, client retention, and multi-location support — positioned as a partner in profitability rather than just software.

⚡ Standout wedge: "Client Retention / Predict" — an AI-driven churn/at-risk client surfacing feature. This is the weird wedge: proactively predicting which members are about to lapse, directly targeting the exact reporting gap OS has named as its headline weakness.

Segment: Boutique fitness studios — yoga, Pilates, barre, Lagree, indoor cycling, HIIT — single and multi-location, including franchises. A segment OS serves but is explicitly NOT optimized for (yoga/spa/general-wellness).

Pricing: Public pricing page exists but tier details not captured in scrape. Positions on the "profitability for all studios" / partner-in-success angle consistent with the category's anti-incumbent, scale-by-size pricing pattern.

Traction: Multiple named studio logos on the homepage (Elevate Training, Sol Seek, Inspire Yoga, River Yoga, Red Light Method, Motivate Movement, LVL Fitness Lagree, etc.); displays NPS / "most likely to recommend" and app-review badges. Mature marketing presence with case studies, testimonials, webinars.

Maturity: Established, well-funded-looking commercial product with full feature marketing, case studies, testimonials, an insurance offering, and a branded-app product — mature within the boutique-studio niche.

Feature set:

  • AI-powered studio management (positioned as the whole pitch, not a checkbox)
  • Client Retention / Predict — churn/at-risk client surfacing
  • Reporting suite
  • Sales & Marketing suite (email/campaigns/CRM)

  • Multi-location management

  • Integrations

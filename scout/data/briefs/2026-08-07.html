HumanGO · Brick-and-mortar · https://humango.ai/

HumanGO is an AI-powered endurance training app centered on "Hugo," a conversational AI coach that builds hyper-personalized, constantly adaptive training plans for endurance athletes (cycling, running, triathlon, swimming, duathlon, etc.). It ingests wearable data, athlete-set goals, weekly availability, and fatigue/recovery signals to auto-generate and continually re-adjust plans around a busy life and calendar. It layers in community groups, progress tracking, and post-workout AI feedback. It also markets "coaching solutions that scale" for coaches and bespoke solutions for teams/events, though those pages are thin.

⚡ Standout wedge: "Hugo," a genuinely conversational AI coach that adapts plans to fatigue/schedule/travel and will "call you out" for slacking — AI as the whole pitch, not a checkbox. The adaptive endurance planning engine (plans that continuously re-optimize around availability, wearables, and goal races) is the weird wedge.

Segment: Endurance/self-coached athletes (B2C) primarily, with a secondary B2B pitch to endurance coaches and teams — bucket (B) online coaching, endurance-vertical specialization. Not a brick-and-mortar facility ops platform.

Pricing: No pricing published on the scraped /pricing page (it renders only a generic "Start Free Trial" CTA). Free trial is the only visible commercial signal; monetization model (subscription tiers) not disclosed on-site.

Traction: Marketing claims "thousands of athletes and counting." Named endorsements from Olympic triathlon coach Lance Watson and cycling coach Pav Bryan, plus a testimonial from Challenge Family's Chairman (Zibi Szlufcik). A long partner logo wall (GTN, HPH Cycling, etc.). Listed on both App Store and Google Play. No hard user/revenue numbers.

Maturity: Live and shipping — dual native apps, active blog/FAQs, endurance-industry partnerships and coach endorsements. Marketing site is athlete-consumer polished but B2B (coaches/teams) and pricing pages are underdeveloped/placeholder, suggesting the coach/team business motion is early-stage relative to the mature consumer app.

Feature set:

  • Hyper-personalized adaptive training plans that auto-adjust to schedule, travel, and performance
  • Weekly availability input so plans fit around a busy life
  • Wearable device connection and training-tool integrations
  • Fatigue / recovery monitoring and health-metric tracking
  • Fitness prediction / progression forecasting
  • Goal & key-race setting with progress tracking
  • Community groups for motivation, support, and shared achievements
  • Multi-discipline support (cycling, running, triathlon, swim, duathlon, aquathlon, MTB, gravel, trail, fitness)
  • Coach-facing 'scale your coaching' low-touch model tooling (thinly described)
  • Team/event/training-group bespoke solutions (thinly described)
  • iOS and Android native apps
  • Free trial onboarding

Gymconnect: Advance Gym Management Software ... · Brick-and-mortar · https://www.producthunt.com/products/gymconnect

Gymconnect is an early-stage, mobile-first gym management platform (launched ~2yr ago as v1 on Product Hunt, India-centric — Razorpay payments, AWS Activate startup credits). It covers core brick-and-mortar ops: membership management, subscriptions, attendance tracking, and user retention. Its distinctive angle is bundling equipment marketplace (buy/sell gym equipment) and lines of credit to finance gym upgrades — i.e. a financial-services/commerce wedge grafted onto standard gym CRM. It tags itself with AI but shows no concrete AI feature in the copy.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge": an equipment buy/sell marketplace plus access to lines of credit to finance gym upgrades — turning the ops tool into a capital/commerce layer for gym owners. Nobody else in the space bakes financing into gym-management software.

Segment: Bucket A — brick-and-mortar general gym/fitness-club ops. General commercial gyms (not S&C/sport-performance specific); India-first market.

Pricing: Free options at launch; promotional 2 months free plus a "1+1 Year subscription post trial" launch offer. No published tiered/flat pricing — fits the anti-incumbent free-tier fingerprint but numbers are undisclosed.

Traction: Very early. First version launched on Product Hunt ~2 years ago with only 6 upvotes on the maker post and 152 followers. No reviews yet ("Be the first to leave a review"). Comments are mostly congratulatory; two commenters directly asked how it differs from MindBody/Classpass/Wodify and received no substantive answer. On AWS Activate credits — signals pre-scale startup.

Maturity: Seed / v1 MVP. Low external validation, no reviews, small following, unclear differentiation vs incumbents. Feature depth appears shallow beyond the financing wedge.

The Best Gym Management Software Platforms: Our 2022 Review · Online coaching · https://twobrainbusiness.com/best-gym-management-software-2021/

This is not a single competitor platform — it's Two-Brain Business's 2022 buyer's-guide review ranking the major gym-management platforms (Mindbody, PushPress, Wodify, Zen Planner, plus a long tail). It's most valuable to TeamBuildr OS as a curated ICP requirements document: it distills what the top ~800 revenue-outperforming gym owners actually demand from software, scores incumbents against those criteria, and surfaces where the whole category is weak. Treat it as a mine of buyer language and unmet needs rather than a feature-for-feature teardown of one product.

⚡ Standout wedge: The review itself names Two-Brain's three "essential" software jobs and calls robust reporting (ARM, LEG, at-risk/last-visit reports, revenue breakdowns) "100 percent essential, not a bonus" — validating OS's headline gap in the buyers' own words. It also flags an industry-wide decline in customer support quality as a live differentiator.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gym/studio ops (bucket A) — the review is written for boutique/functional-fitness gym owners, heavily CrossFit-adjacent (Wodify, SugarWOD, WOD-logging references), which overlaps OS's S&C ICP.

Pricing: Mindbody (the profiled leader) runs $159–$699/mo across tiers with many features gated to upper tiers or paid add-ons (VOD, branded app, reporting upgrades). The review implicitly favors platforms that reduce cost by covering multiple functions rather than forcing linked subscriptions.

Traction: High authority: sourced from ~800 top-performing Two-Brain gym owners plus the 6,100-member Gym Owners United group (est. $7.5M/yr software spend, 700,000+ members). Usage polls show PushPress, Wodify, Zen Planner leading among this segment; Mindbody declining. Notably, 14% of Two-Brain respondents were actively intending to switch platforms — a live churn/switching signal in OS's target market.

Maturity: Mature-market review of established incumbents; the guide is a recurring annual publication (2021, 2022, 2024 editions) from a respected mentorship org — a stable, high-trust buyer-education asset.

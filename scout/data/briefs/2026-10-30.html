kmura · Brick-and-mortar · https://kmura.com/

Kmura is a BJJ-specific gym management platform ("operating system for your academy") built around nightly AI alerts across six data streams (financial, leads, churn, engagement, promotions, schedule). It combines CRM, billing, belt/stripe/curriculum tracking, student portal, lead pipeline, communications, and multi-location support — positioned explicitly as the vertical-native replacement for Zen Planner, Kicksite, BJJLink, and Gymdesk.

⚡ Standout wedge: The weird wedge is deep BJJ vertical nativeness fused with full Portuguese (PT-BR) localization top to bottom — owner dashboard, staff tools, AND student portal switch languages with one click — plus nightly AI alerts packaged as the whole pitch rather than a checkbox.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar BJJ / jiu-jitsu academy operators (single and multi-location), primarily USA & Canada with a strong Brazilian-Portuguese bilingual community angle.

Pricing: $150/month flat per location, unlimited students, everything included except SMS (SMS: one-time $20 reg + $5/mo + per-text cost). Card processing 2.6% + 30¢ with no monthly payments fee; ACH $1.25 flat up to $100 then 1.25%. No contracts. First 2 months free via promo code. Explicitly anti-incumbent: beats competitor processing rates and scale-by-location-not-feature pricing.

Traction: Testimonials from academies across USA & Canada, several multi-location (3-location) owners, and world-champion BJJ ambassadors (Lucas Araujo, Vinicius Dos Reis, Desiree Favoreto) using it to run their academies. Heavy "switched from another platform" social proof. Appears to be an early but credibly-adopted vertical product.

Maturity: Early-stage but feature-complete vertical platform — a deep, polished BJJ-native product rather than an MVP. AI alerts are live and shipping, migration/onboarding is operational.

Feature set:

  • Nightly AI alerts across six streams: financial, leads, churn, engagement, promotions, schedule — with drafted win-back/follow-up messages
  • AI Overview Dashboard with churn-risk, cold-lead, promotion-eligibility and financial (failed charges, past-due, disputes) surfacing
  • Leads pipeline (Kanban/list), lead forms, landing pages with embed code, trial scheduling, pre-join waivers
  • Communications & automations: one-time + triggered email/SMS campaigns, two-way SMS inbox, minor-messages routed to guardian
  • Belt/stripe tracking with fully customizable curriculum, lesson plans, promotion eligibility by classes + time-at-rank
  • Instructional video library gated by program/belt/plan, attachable to classes, optionally paid, required-for-promotion
  • Billing & memberships: card + ACH, family plans, pause/freeze, scheduled cancellations, prorated refunds, per-student overrides
  • Dual pricing / pass card fees to students where allowed
  • Financials with AI past-due recovery assistant, disputes/chargebacks with evidence upload, CSV/PDF export
  • Reports: financial, tax, retention, conversion funnel, attendance analytics, rank distribution, waiver compliance; period comparison, saved, scheduled email, XLSX export
  • Check-in kiosk (PIN, name, QR, barcode, phone, printed student cards)
  • Events with public registration, early-bird tiers, automatic member pricing, built-in payments
  • Merchandise / inventory with student-portal storefront
  • Multi-location: separate legal entities + bank accounts per location, home gym per student, cross-location attendance
  • Full English + Brazilian Portuguese across owner, staff, and student experiences
  • Free white-glove full-history migration (attendance, promotions, payments, waivers)
  • Referral program — one free month per referred gym, no cap

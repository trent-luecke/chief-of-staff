coachway · Brick-and-mortar · https://coachway.io/

Coachway is an online coaching platform aimed at fitness/nutrition coaches, pitching itself as an all-in-one workspace for meal planning, client communication, and daily coaching workflows. Its headline promise is efficiency — "coaches save 50% of their time weekly after switching to Coachway." It leans heavily on a Nordic-first identity: 6 languages (Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish, German, English) and a wall of Scandinavian influencer-coach social proof.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is deep Nordic/European localization — the product is natively built and marketed in 6 languages with a dense roster of Scandinavian influencer-coach social proof, positioning it as the default for the underserved Nordic online-coaching market rather than another US-centric tool.

Segment: Online coaching (bucket B) — solo fitness/nutrition coaches and influencer-coaches, concentrated in the Nordics/DACH region rather than brick-and-mortar facilities.

Pricing: Not published on the scraped page. Offers a 14-day free trial, "cancel anytime," and a free demo; no dollar figure or tier structure shown.

Traction: Claims "5.0 on Capterra" and displays a large wall (60+) of named coach clients, several with substantial social followings (e.g., 202K, 139K, 78.7K), heavily Nordic. Signals real but early, regionally-concentrated traction; company appears young.

Maturity: Emerging / early-stage. Marketing frames itself as the "new" platform coaches are "switching to," suggesting a challenger still building out; concentrated in one geographic niche.

xoda · Brick-and-mortar · https://xoda.com/

XODA is an all-in-one gym and fitness management platform built for gyms, studios, yoga/pilates, martial arts, PTs and wellness businesses. It bundles ten deeply-integrated modules (memberships, class scheduling, online bookings, payments, CRM, staff management, branded member app, reporting, marketing and community) into a single system. Strongly rooted in Australia/NZ with expansion across UK, US and Asia, it positions itself as the modern, faster, cheaper replacement for legacy incumbents (Mindbody, GloFox, Zen Planner, etc.).

⚡ Standout wedge: The weird wedge is a full AI SKU tier ($449/mo "AI" plan bundling AI assistant/chatbots, XODA Insights advanced reporting, branded white-label app and 24/7 access control) plus a freemium-to-enterprise ladder that scales by business size, not feature-gating the core ops. AI is packaged as a named plan, exactly matching the category fingerprint.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gym/studio operations (bucket A) — spanning single-location studios to multi-site/franchise operators, across gyms, 24-hour gyms, fitness/yoga/pilates studios, martial arts, PT and recovery centres.

Pricing: Freemium ($0 forever: web portal + member app, unlimited staff/members, automated billing, check-in, POS, basic reporting) → Growth ($379+tax/mo: staff app, lead pipelines, marketing automation, advanced membership builder, booking widgets, promo/referral) → AI ($449+tax/mo: white-label app, AI assistant/chatbots, XODA Insights, access control, group messaging, 500 SMS/mo) → Enterprise (POA: multi-site, tailored onboarding, priority support). Anti-incumbent "transparent, no hidden fees" framing; free tier as the wedge.

Traction: Claims 500+ gyms & studios, 500k+ members managed, across 11 countries, 100% Australian-based support. Named customer testimonials (Rehub Wellness, Code5 Fitness, Studio X, FitMiss, Empowa Women Fitness, Sunrise Fitness) with growth stats (e.g. 20% YoY). Aggressively publishes head-to-head comparison pages vs. 11 incumbents.

Maturity: Established and commercially mature for a modern challenger — real customer base across 11 countries, full 10-module product breadth, multiple pricing tiers including a live AI SKU, and an extensive comparison/switching-migration motion. Iterating fast on features per customer quotes.

Feature set:

  • Membership management (recurring plans, contracts, freezes, upgrades, renewals, class packs)
  • Class scheduling with capacity, waitlists and recurring sessions
  • AI smart scheduling to fill classes
  • 24/7 self-service online bookings across web and mobile
  • Payments (direct debit, cards, in-person) via Ezypay, WorldPay, Stripe with reconciliation, refunds, failed-payment management
  • Member CRM with lead pipelines and prospect/trial/member stages
  • Staff management (rosters, payroll, permissions, performance) + XODA Go staff app
  • Branded (white-label) member mobile app on App Store / Google Play
  • Reporting & analytics (revenue, retention, utilisation) + XODA Insights AI reporting
  • Marketing automation — email, SMS, campaigns, promo & referral codes, speed-to-lead workflows
  • Community — in-app feeds, challenges, app-to-app messaging and group chats
  • 24/7 access control integrations / secure self check-in
  • Multi-site management for groups and franchises
  • Point of sale (POS) / retail
  • Migration support (data moved within ~24 hours)
  • Workout tracking

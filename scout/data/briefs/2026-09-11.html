membershipsoftware · Brick-and-mortar · https://membershipsoftware.online/

A generic, white-label "membership management" SaaS positioned for clubs, groups, associations, and societies — not fitness-specific. Sells automation, cloud document management, real-time analytics, and customizable membership/subscription management with an OpenAI support layer and n8n automation. Copy is boilerplate ("Empower Your Business to Thrive"), the core-features section repeats the same placeholder card five times, and /features and /pricing subpages 404, suggesting an early/template-stage product.

⚡ Standout wedge: Pricing scales purely by size (user/member/plan limits) with all features included at every paid tier, plus a bundled OpenAI support layer and n8n automation as gated add-ons — automation/AI packaged into low-cost yearly tiers ($50–$250/yr).

Segment: Generic membership/subscription management for clubs, groups, associations, and societies — industry-agnostic, not built for fitness or S&C.

Pricing: Free "Demo" tier (2 users, 5 members, AI/n8n disabled). Paid yearly tiers scale by size only, all features included: Micro $50/yr (500 members) → Super $250/yr (5,000 members). AI (OpenAI) and n8n unlock at the first paid tier; coupons only at Super. /pricing page 404s — pricing lives on the homepage.

Traction: Self-reported 500+ customers, 11+ languages, 4+ subscription plans. No named logos, testimonials, or founder story. Template-grade site with repeated placeholder cards and 404 subpages signals a very early / low-maturity product.

Maturity: Early-stage / template-grade. Boilerplate copy, five identical duplicated feature cards, and 404 /features and /pricing pages indicate a nascent or white-label build.

Feature set:

  • Membership & subscription management (member records, membership plans)
  • End-to-end workflow automation / n8n integration
  • OpenAI-powered support/AI layer
  • Real-time analytics and performance tracking
  • Cloud document storage (paperless workflows, encrypted)
  • Coupon/discount management (top tier only)
  • Logged history / audit trail
  • Multi-language support (11+ languages)
  • Customizable solutions and third-party integrations
  • 24/7 customer support

OS-tagged takeaways:

  • ✅ We do this — Membership & subscription management (records, plans): OS core facility ops covers membership management. This is table-stakes and generic here — no fitness/S&C specificity.
  • 🎯 Real gap — OpenAI-powered AI support layer: Native AI is a gap but actively in progress via the Workflow Builder Claude layer. Not a blind spot — tag as gap in progress.
  • 🎯 Real gap — n8n / end-to-end automation: Maps to OS's integrations/automation gap — Zapier shipped but DOA; the OS Workflow Builder is the in-progress answer. Their bundled n8n is the pattern to watch.
  • 🎯 Real gap — Real-time analytics / performance tracking: Reporting is the OS headline gap (no at-risk/churn, granular revenue, per-class economics). But this platform's 'analytics' is generic boilerplate with no fitness specificity, so limited idea-mining value beyond confirming the category expectation.
  • ❓ Your call — Cloud document storage / paperless workflows: Doc-management for clubs/associations doesn't map cleanly to the S&C facility-ops profile — flag rather than guess.
  • ❓ Your call — Coupon / discount management: Promo/coupon codes aren't named in the grounding as an ICP pain or an existing OS capability. Flag for Trent.
  • 🚫 Out of scope — Multi-language support (11+ languages): Localization is this platform's 'weird wedge.' OS ICP is US S&C facilities; broad localization is a bells-and-whistles bet OS hasn't chosen.
  • 🚫 Out of scope — Coupon gated to top tier + AI/n8n gated behind paid tiers: Feature-gating by tier runs opposite to OS's transparent single-price, no-feature-gating model — deliberately not OS's approach.

JTBD: Give any membership-based organization (club, association, society) a generic, customizable tool to automate member management, ditch paperwork, and run operations from the cloud — 'Empower Your Business to Thrive.' The job is horizontal admin automation, not fitness programming or facility coaching.

Verdict: ➖ Different job — This is an industry-agnostic membership-admin tool for clubs/associations, not an S&C or sport-performance facility platform. No programming, no training, no fitness ICP — it sits outside OS's confirmed JTBD (consolidation + programming depth for S&C facilities). Note briefly and move on.

rhinofit · Brick-and-mortar · https://rhinofit.ca/

RhinoFit is a flat-rate ($57/mo) gym management software from a small Clearwater, Florida team, in market since 2012. It handles billing, class scheduling, waivers, POS, belt tracking, email/SMS, and 24/7 door-access hardware for independent gyms, CrossFit boxes, dojos, and sports clubs. Its entire pitch is anti-incumbent pricing plus radically human customer service ("real people answer the phone").

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is trust-through-humanity: a 14-year-old founder-operated shop where the CEO still writes code and takes calls, a named-humans support team you can phone, and a dead-simple flat $57 unlimited-members price with no usage fees and no per-member tax on growth.

Segment: Independent brick-and-mortar gyms, CrossFit boxes, dojos/martial arts, and sports clubs in the US and Canada — small operators, including true startups (free $0 tier for ≤20 users).

Pricing: Flat $57/month, unlimited members, no tiers and no per-member fees; email & SMS included with no usage fee. Free 30-day trial (no card). Free $0/mo start-up plan for 20 or fewer active users. 24/7 door access and branded app are paid add-ons. No contract.

Traction: 4.8★ across 201 Google reviews; operating since 2012 (14 years); Capterra + several service/UX/fast-implementation awards. Small single-building team in Clearwater, FL. Reviews heavily emphasize customer service.

Maturity: Mature, stable niche player — 14 years in market, small but steady, established review base and award history. Not high-growth/VC-scale; deliberately small.

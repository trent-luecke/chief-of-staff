wodboard · Brick-and-mortar · https://wodboard.com/

WodBoard is a UK-based all-in-one gym management platform aimed at class-based and PT gyms (heavily CrossFit affiliate-oriented). It bundles core gym ops (payments, bookings, comms, reporting) with a CRM, done-for-you ad management, built-for-you gym websites, and a white-label branded member app. Positioning leans hard on consolidation ("the only gym software you'll ever need"), human support from people who've run gyms, mobile-first operation, and low-friction switching/migration.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is done-for-you marketing as a native product — WodBoard Ads (fully managed ad campaigns with copy/creative/launch handled by their team, no agency retainer) plus built-for-you gym websites, bundled into the same platform as ops. Marketing-as-a-service, not just marketing software.

Segment: Class-based and PT (personal training) gym owners, concentrated in the UK CrossFit affiliate market. Overlaps OS's facility-ops ICP but skews toward CrossFit-native / class-booking operations and marketing-funnel-hungry owners rather than periodized S&C programming depth.

Pricing: No public per-seat/flat price shown on the scraped pages. Promotional framing only (Black Friday waitlist: "join now and pay nothing until February"). Sales-led via "Book a demo" / "Speak to an Expert." All-in-one with modular add/remove of products ("add products as you need them").

Traction: Multiple named UK CrossFit affiliate customers with testimonials (CrossFit Glasgow, Leicester, Warminster, Stormbreaker, Newquay, Stirling, Vauxhall, Hull & Humber, plus Percheron Fitness, Back Beach, Dragon Athletic, etc.). Ratings badges across Capterra, GetApp, App Store, Play Store, Software Advice. Self-reported stats: +29.2% average gym growth (2023), 27 hrs admin saved/month, 72% increase in member engagement, 120-min average migration.

Maturity: Established and actively shipping — long-tenured customers (one cites 16 years), broad review-site presence, and a mature multi-product suite. Credible mid-market operator in the UK gym-ops space, iterating quickly per customer testimonials about responsiveness.

Feature set:

  • Gym management core: recurring membership billing (auto-collected, no chasing), class/1:1/course bookings on a drag-and-drop timetable, comms, reporting
  • Gym CRM: lead boards + list views with filtering, email/SMS/WhatsApp triggers, tasks and staff alerts, AI and automation built in
  • WodBoard Ads: done-for-you ad management (campaign build, copy, creative, launch, budget control)
  • Gym websites: professionally designed, mobile-fast, SEO-oriented, brand-customized
  • White-label branded member app (gym's own name, logo, colors; members book, buy, track)
  • At-risk / churn surfacing ('you spot them drifting first' — flags the 3-5% of members quietly leaving)
  • Mobile-first: full admin and member functionality on phone (parity with desktop)
  • In-app AI or bring-your-own-AI connection
  • Human support from people who've run gyms; migration service (export/import, member invites, data/payment carryover, onboarding & training in ~120 min)

AdviceFit: AI Health Platform & Gym Management Software · Brick-and-mortar · https://advice.fit/

AdviceFit is an India-built "one AI health platform" from AIONDATA that spans individuals, trainers, gyms, studios, wellness businesses, and multi-location operators. It bundles gym management (memberships, billing, biometric attendance, WhatsApp engagement), a member health app (workouts, nutrition, progress), a coaching workspace, and AI-assisted business intelligence — all in one connected platform with India-ready depth (UPI, WhatsApp, biometrics) plus USD international plans.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is deep India-native operating depth — UPI payment journeys, WhatsApp-led renewal/reactivation workflows, and device-specific biometric attendance (ESSL, ZKTeud, Realtime) proven across 1,000+ gyms via sister product AionHRMS — packaged as localization-first gym software that then travels internationally.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gym/studio ops with a coaching + personal-health layer, straddling into online coaching. Primary market India + emerging markets (Malaysia, Singapore, Sri Lanka) with international USD reach; segments from solo individuals up to 20+ center multi-location chains.

Pricing: Freemium ₹0 (25 members / 5 staff). India tiers scale by member count: Basic ₹900/mo (150 members), Professional ₹1,000/mo (500 members), Enterprise ₹1,500/mo (2,500 members, API + white-label), Enterprise+ custom (unlimited). International gym plans start at US$25/mo. Separate plans for Gyms/Providers, Trainers/Coaches, and Individuals. Tiers do gate some features (broadcast messaging, advanced analytics, API, white-label).

Traction: Biometric attendance engine proven across 1,000+ gyms (shared with AionHRMS). Enterprise trust signals: ISO 9001:2015 certified, CMMI Level 3 appraised, GDPR ready, NVIDIA Inception member. Named customer profile (The Hype Gym multi-center). Backed by AIONDATA parent with sibling products (AionHRMS, AionCRM).

Maturity: Established, operationally mature product — enterprise-grade certifications, a large biometric install base via the parent company, published pricing across multiple countries, extensive SEO/guide content, and comparison pages vs. regional competitors. Clearly a shipping, revenue-generating platform rather than early-stage.

Gym Insight: Gym Management Software · Brick-and-mortar · https://gyminsight.com/

Gym Insight is an all-in-one gym management software for brick-and-mortar fitness businesses, centering on front-desk operations: POS/hardware, member management, billing/payment processing, class scheduling, access control, and a sales/CRM tool. Positioned as "the easiest-to-use" platform with flat-monthly pricing (no percentage cut) and a strong facility-ops + access-control emphasis.

⚡ Standout wedge: Native gym access control (24/7 security, digital/barcode/RFID key tags) paired with modern POS hardware for the front desk — a physical-facility operations depth OS deliberately lacks.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gyms and fitness clubs — new club owners, existing club owners, and personal trainers. Bucket (A) gym/studio ops. General facility operations focus, not S&C programming.

Pricing: Flat monthly fee only, "zero percentages" — no payment-processing percentage cut. Anti-incumbent pricing angle. Exact dollar amount not disclosed on scraped pages; directs to a pricing page / "Try Insight."

Traction: No traction metrics (customer counts, funding, reviews) visible on the scraped pages. Segmented onboarding paths (new owners, existing owners, personal trainers) and a modern Webflow site suggest an established, actively-marketed product.

Maturity: Established, full-featured gym-ops product — broad feature set spanning POS, billing, access control, scheduling, and CRM indicates a mature operations platform rather than an early-stage tool.

Feature set:

  • POS hardware + invoicing (scan-to-sale, receipts, sell memberships/merch/drinks/services)

  • Sales/POS reporting (what's moving, revenue opportunities)
  • Sales Tool with built-in CRM, follow-ups, and real-time performance tracking
  • Member management (account overview, check-in software, retention contact tools)
  • Class scheduling with app/website booking, calendar display, drop-in rates, easy reservations

  • Member mobile app

  • Flat monthly pricing, zero percentages

cloudfit · Brick-and-mortar · https://cloudfit.tv/

CloudFit is workout display / digital signage software for gyms and studios. It brings exercise videos, timers, digital whiteboards and branded instructions to in-gym TV screens for both coached classes and self-directed training. The pitch is a "next-level workout experience" delivered consistently on-screen — replacing physical whiteboards and freeing trainers from repetitive instruction so they can coach the room. It is explicitly NOT a gym management/CRM platform: no booking, billing, membership, scheduling (beyond auto-running workouts on screens), or member CRM. It's a single-purpose in-facility delivery/display layer that sits alongside whatever management stack a gym already runs.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is the entire product: in-gym workout display/signage as a dedicated category — turning any TV + ~$80-150 media device into a branded, timer-driven, video-guided workout screen that replaces whiteboards. It's a delivery-layer play (not CRM), funded by an aggressive free tier.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gyms, boutique studios, leisure/rec centers, and franchises (Anytime Fitness, Snap, UFC Gym, YMCA logos) across HIIT/functional, strength & conditioning, Pilates/yoga, martial arts/boxing, and mixed-modality formats — any facility running coached classes or self-directed training on screens.

Pricing: Free tier (single display, full builder, brand customization, 5 custom video uploads, 2 published workouts) with no contracts or time limits. Business at $149/mo per site (up to 15 displays, unlimited scheduling, CloudFit AI, 10,000 custom videos, priority support, 30-day free trial). Custom Multisite/Enterprise pricing for 10+ sites; special Education/Non-Profit pricing. Anti-incumbent free-tier + flat-per-site model; hardware is cheap off-the-shelf consumer media devices.

Traction: Strong logo wall including major franchises (Anytime Fitness, Snap Fitness, UFC Gym, Jetts, YMCA, Viva Leisure) plus dozens of boutique studios/boxes across US/UK/Australia. Numerous enthusiastic named testimonials. "Powering Fitness Worldwide" counters render as 0+ (placeholder/unpopulated on scrape). Positions directly against Aktiv TV, MZ-Instruct, PEAR, Les Mills, Wexer with migration guides — signaling an established, actively-selling product in the display niche.

Maturity: Established and polished within its niche — mature workout-display product with tiered pricing, enterprise hardware options, migration guides from legacy competitors, and a sizable customer roster. Not a management platform, so "maturity" is scoped to the display/delivery category.

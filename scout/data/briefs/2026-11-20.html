legitfit · Brick-and-mortar · https://legitfit.com/

LegitFit is an all-in-one gym management software for studios, boxes, PTs, and multi-location fitness/wellness businesses. It bundles scheduling, memberships/packages, payments, check-in (with turnstile integration), financial reporting, client health/progress tracking, exercise programming with video, automated messaging/CRM-style engagement, branded apps/emails, and third-party integrations.

⚡ Standout wedge: Enhanced, dynamic financial reporting (MRR, gross/net volume trend lines) paired with client "health scores" — surfacing exactly the revenue-granularity and at-risk-member signals that are OS's headline reporting gap.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gym/studio ops (Bucket A) — broad horizontal coverage across gyms, yoga/pilates/CrossFit studios, PTs, SGPT, leisure centers, and hotels.

Pricing: Not publicly published — gated behind "Get demo & pricing." Black Friday promo of 3 months free. Trust signal: "Trusted by 7,000+ fitness professionals."

Traction: Claims 7,000+ fitness professionals; logo wall of studios/boxes (Tribe Gym, Balance, Eventus22, MTU Arena, Strive, The Engine Room, etc.), skewing EUR-denominated (European/Irish market).

Maturity: Established, full-featured all-in-one — mature breadth across ops, programming, reporting, and member engagement.

Feature set:

  • Flexible timetable for classes, 1:1 appointments, sessions and programs
  • Custom memberships, packages, and discounted plans with multiple payment methods
  • Member check-in with QR codes and turnstile integration (access control)
  • Dynamic financial reports — MRR, gross volume, net volume with year-over-year trends
  • Client health scores — progress, engagement, attendance, no-shows, cancellations
  • Waitlist push notifications to raise full-class rates
  • Third-party integrations via Zapier (3,000+), CRMs, marketing tools
  • Staff performance monitoring and payroll (hours, bookings, cancellations)
  • Exercise program builder with blocks, sets, reps, weights, RPE
  • Collaborative client goal setting and progress tracking
  • Video tutorials embedded in programs
  • Automated message builder (triggers like client-added)
  • Milestone celebration messages for retention
  • Branded member app on App Store / Google Play
  • Branded emails
  • Coupons / promo codes
  • One-to-one Message Hub
  • Broadcast SMS and email campaigns

getmilton · Brick-and-mortar · https://getmilton.com/

Milton is an all-in-one gym/coaching management platform whose entire pitch is centered on a named AI coach ("Hire Milton"). It consolidates payments, scheduling, workout programming, member messaging, CRM and reporting into one workspace, while packaging AI coaching as a new revenue stream — members get a 24/7 AI coach, trainers get AI assistants for programming, progress reports, check-ins and call prep, and the gym gets upsell paths into personal training and premium memberships.

⚡ Standout wedge: The "weird wedge" is positioning AI coaching as a revenue stream, not a feature — "Don't buy more software. Hire Milton. Make money with AI Coaching." The AI is both member-facing (24/7 coach, meal photo logging, Q&A) and trainer-facing (drafts programs, progress reports, check-in nudges, call prep), framed as added gym revenue rather than a cost line.

Segment: Brick-and-mortar gyms, boxes, studios, training/wellness facilities, and independent coaches (straddles both ops and online-coaching buckets).

Pricing: No public price on the scraped pages — gated behind a 3-question qualifier that routes to a free trial link (by text or email) or a "Growth Call" demo. Monetization framing leans on gyms making money via AI coaching upsells rather than transparent subscription tiers.

Traction: Logo wall of ~11 named gyms/boxes ("Optimal Performance," "Johnny O's Gymnasium," "The Dungeon," "Elevated Fitness KC," etc.) claimed "across the country." Several core modules still tagged Coming soon / Early access (CRM, Workflows, device integrations), suggesting an early-stage product still building out its stack.

Maturity: Early-stage / launching. Core ops exist but CRM is "Coming soon," Workflows "Early access," and device integrations "Coming soon"; AI examples are explicitly labeled product previews ("Sample members and sample responses"). Reads as a young, AI-first product mid-buildout.

Feature set:

  • Named AI coach (member-facing, 24/7 chat for meals, recipes, workout help)
  • AI meal logging via photo (calories + macros)
  • AI-generated workout programs and periodized plan drafts (RPE-based blocks)
  • AI progress reports and nutrition-guidance drafts for trainers
  • AI check-in/at-risk surfacing (members who haven't logged a workout / sleep down / missed sessions)
  • AI call-prep briefs before coaching sessions
  • AI-drafted text reminders and win-back flows
  • Payments / billing / memberships / packages / payouts
  • Class & session scheduling with rooms and coaches
  • Workout Builder / exercise & program library
  • Member CRM (Coming soon) + lead pipeline stages
  • Workflows — automated texts and emails (Early access)
  • Reports dashboard (how the gym is doing)
  • Member mobile app (workouts, nutrition, progress, body comp, progress photos)
  • Wearable / smart scale / sleep device integrations (Coming soon)
  • Files, forms and waivers
  • Guided migration/switchover service (map, set up, test, phased go-live)
  • ROI calculator (software savings, staff time, new revenue)
  • PT upsell / premium-membership-upgrade monetization tooling

A — Explicit Pickers

Separate person search + task search fields. Always visible, no guessing. Click the field, type to search, select. Works well when you know who/what you're linking to before you write.

B — @mention inline

Type @name anywhere in the note body to link a person. Type #task or /task to link a task. No separate fields — linking happens naturally as you write. Faster but relies on remembering the syntax.

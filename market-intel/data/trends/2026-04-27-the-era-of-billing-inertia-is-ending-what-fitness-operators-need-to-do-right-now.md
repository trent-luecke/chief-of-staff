ABC Fitness is highlighting a major industry shift where Visa and other payment processors are making it easier for consumers to cancel subscriptions, ending the era where gyms could rely on billing friction to retain members. This trend directly impacts retention strategies for all gym management software platforms including TeamBuildr OS, as gyms will need better engagement and value delivery rather than relying on cancellation difficulty.

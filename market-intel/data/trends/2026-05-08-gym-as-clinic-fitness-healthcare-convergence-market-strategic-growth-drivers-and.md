Industry research report examining the convergence of fitness and healthcare services, with gyms increasingly positioning themselves as health clinics. This trend toward medical integration could impact how independent gyms differentiate themselves and what services they need to track and manage. The 10-year outlook suggests this will be a sustained industry shift rather than a passing trend.

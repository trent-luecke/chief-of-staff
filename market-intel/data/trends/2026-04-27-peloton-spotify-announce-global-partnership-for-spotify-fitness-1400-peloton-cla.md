Peloton partnered with Spotify to offer 1,400+ Peloton fitness classes to Spotify Premium subscribers through a new Spotify Fitness feature. This partnership expands Peloton's reach beyond their hardware ecosystem to tap into Spotify's massive subscriber base. While Peloton primarily focuses on at-home fitness rather than gym management, this move shows how fitness content providers are finding new distribution channels.

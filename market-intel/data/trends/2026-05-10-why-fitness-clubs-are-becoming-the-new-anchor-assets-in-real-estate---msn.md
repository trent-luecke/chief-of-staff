Fitness clubs are increasingly being viewed as anchor tenants in real estate developments, similar to grocery stores or department stores. This trend suggests growing recognition of fitness facilities as stable, traffic-driving businesses that can anchor retail and mixed-use developments. For TeamBuildr OS, this indicates potential growth opportunities for their target market of independent gym owners as real estate developers may be more willing to lease to fitness businesses.

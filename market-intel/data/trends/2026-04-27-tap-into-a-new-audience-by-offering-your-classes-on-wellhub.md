TeamUp announced an integration with Wellhub (formerly Gympass) that allows fitness venues to sync their classes and locations to Wellhub's corporate wellness platform. This partnership gives TeamUp customers access to a new revenue stream through corporate wellness clients, potentially making TeamUp more attractive to gym owners who want to tap into the B2B wellness market.

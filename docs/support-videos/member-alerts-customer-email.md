# Member Alerts launch email (current OS customers)

**Send after** production is confirmed live on launch day (Wed 10/28). Customers will click straight into Reports, so the feature has to be there when they do.

**Audience:** owners and admins. Front desk staff can't see Overview or Reports, so don't send this to front-desk-only users.

---

**Subject options**
1. See who's slipping away before they cancel
2. New in TeamBuildr OS: Member Alerts
3. Your Overview page just got more useful

**Preview text:** A new report flags at-risk members, and your Overview page now shows today's schedule up top.

---

Hi {{first_name}},

Most cancellations don't come out of nowhere. A member stops showing up, their class pack runs out, or their package quietly expires, and by the time you notice, they've already decided. Starting today, TeamBuildr OS points those members out for you.

**New: the Member alerts report**

Go to **Reports → Member alerts** to see every member who has signed in during the last 90 days. The members who need the most attention are listed first. Each one is flagged with color-coded alerts:

- **Red (act now):** no sign-in for 14 days, or no active membership or package
- **Yellow (act soon):** a class pack down to 3 credits or fewer, or a package expiring within 2 weeks
- **Grey (good to know):** attendance down from last week or last month, or on pause

You'll also see each member's sign-ins for the last four weeks, their cancellations, and how long it's been since their last visit. When you reach out, click **Add note** to log it. Your whole team can see the note, so nobody calls the same member twice.

**Refreshed: your Overview page**

The Overview page now answers two questions: what's happening today, and who needs your attention.

- **Today's schedule** runs across the top: every class, appointment and workshop still to come today.
- **Member alerts** shows the 25 members with the most alerts. Click **View full report** to see everyone.
- **Your totals** for revenue, attendance and new members have moved to **Reports → General**.

**Watch the walkthroughs:** [Your new Overview page](https://support.os.teambuildr.com/article/0tpcowx51f-staff-overview-page) · [The Member alerts report](https://support.os.teambuildr.com/article/13kfrdya43-member-alerts-report-overview)

We'd suggest checking Member alerts once a week. A five-minute look is often enough to save a membership.

Questions? Just reply to this email.

{{sender_name}}
TeamBuildr OS

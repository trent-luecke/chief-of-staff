## Purpose

Per-competitor comparison pages positioning **TeamBuildr OS** against gym/studio management platforms. One page per competitor — not a single master grid.

**Competitor set (7):** MindBody, Walla, Glofox, ZenPlanner (+SugarWOD), PushPress, Wodify, Exercise.com.

## The strategic frame (why this isn't a normal comparison chart)

OS is **intentionally feature-light and operationally focused.** A traditional feature-to-feature grid rewards feature *count* and would make OS look like the wrong choice by design. So these pages **change the axes of comparison** — experience, focus, economics, and fit rather than feature quantity. On those axes, a focused product beats a bloated one.

**The spine of every page:** OS handles everything that touches a paying member — booking, billing, scheduling, POS, staff reporting, flexible schedule items — and does it cleanly, then integrates with the marketing/lead tools you already trust. Focused instrument vs. junk drawer.

**OS facts (confirmed with Trent — provable claims):**
- **One member app.** Members use a *single* app for booking, scheduling, billing, **and** workout tracking (OS + Strength combo). This is the flagship differentiator.
- **In scope:** member booking/billing/scheduling; staff reporting, POS, flexible schedule items; native workout delivery/programming (TeamBuildr S&C heritage).
- **Deliberately NOT in scope:** lead management, native marketing (email workflows, campaigns, CRM). Owned as a *stance* — see "What OS leaves to the specialists."
- **Pricing:** $200/mo or $2,000/yr (~17% annual savings), published. OS + Strength bundle ≈ 20% off (varies by Strength tier).
- **Payments:** Stripe-native; OS takes **0% cut** of processing fees. Only revenue is the subscription.
- **Contracts:** Month-to-month (cancel anytime) or annual.

**Honest buyer qualifier (bake in, don't hide):** OS is for gyms whose lead-gen lives elsewhere. A gym that wants one login for *leads + ops + marketing* is not the buyer.

**Transparent pricing is a differentiator — but only against the quote-only crowd.** OS publishes its price; MindBody, Glofox, and Exercise.com are quote-only ("book a demo to hear the price"). Lean into it there. **NOT against Walla, PushPress, or Wodify — they publish pricing too.**

**Guardrail — no glass-house attacks.** Never attack a competitor on something OS shares. Specifically: OS + Strength is two separate subscriptions with two invoices, so we do NOT criticize "two products / two invoices / an integration between them." (The *member app* is still one app — that claim is fine.) Every attack line must be one OS itself passes.

## Sourcing conventions (READ — governs what may go public)

Each competitor cell is one of:
- **Plain text** = CONFIRMED from the competitor's official source. Safe to publish.
- **"(reported)"** = third-party/operator-sourced only, NOT on the competitor's official site. Must be attributed on the page ("per operator reviews…") or cut. These are the claims that draw legal challenge.
- **"—" / omitted** = NOT FOUND from any reliable source. Do not guess; leave blank.
- **⚑** = a specific item RevOps must re-verify before publish.

Prices and promos change — **RevOps re-pulls every competitor pricing/contract cell at publish time and dates it.**

## Two page archetypes

Deciding fact: **does the member get a single-app experience that includes programming?** If not → they need a second app (or have no programming) → OS's one-app hero lands. If yes → different wedge.

### Archetype A — "Second app / no programming" → one-app hero
**MindBody, Walla, Glofox.** All confirmed: no native workout programming. MindBody and Glofox deliver programming only via the **Trainerize** integration (a second app); Walla has none (class booking + streaming video only). Glofox also gets "independent, not enterprise" (ABC Fitness).

### Archetype B — "Has unified programming" → other wedges
**PushPress, Wodify, Exercise.com** (native programming in one branded app) and **ZenPlanner + SugarWOD** (programming via SugarWOD; single-app status unverified). One-app hero doesn't apply. Compete on depth, fit, pricing model, and processing economics. **No "built by gym owners" claim here** — PushPress/Wodify own that ground.

## Archetype B pages (design deliberately — no one-app hero, no "built by gym owners" claim)

### Page 4 — OS vs. PushPress

| Row | **TeamBuildr OS** | **PushPress** |
|---|---|---|
| **Built for** | Sport-performance coaches | CrossFit / functional-fitness boxes (CrossFit-recommended) |
| **Programming depth** | Periodized, %-based S&C programming | Native "Train" ($79 add-on), same app — WOD/benchmark/day-unlock, not periodized |
| **How we make money** | **0% cut of Stripe fees** | Stripe required; **processing markup tiered by plan — free tier 4.99% + $0.30 CC**, Pro 2.89%, Max 2.75% |

| **Contracts** | Month-to-month or annual | Month-to-month |

**Washes (do NOT contest):** "built by gym owners" (their founders owned boxes), published pricing, month-to-month, Stripe-native. **Hero:** lead with programming depth or processing economics, not app count.

### Page 7 — OS vs. ZenPlanner (+ SugarWOD)

ZenPlanner (Daxko) = member management / back-office; **SugarWOD** (Daxko) = the programming/community layer, integrated via Automatic Member Sync.

| Row | **TeamBuildr OS** | **ZenPlanner + SugarWOD** |
|---|---|---|

**Publish-safety:** Daxko ownership, processor-steering, SugarWOD CrossFit focus = CONFIRMED. ZenPlanner tier prices = reported → present as "quote-based, ~$99–$300 by member count." SugarWOD per-athlete = CONFIRMED model.
